    Solution:

    10.

    Find \(f'(x)\) for
    \begin{equation*} f(x)=\frac{19\sec\mathopen{}\left(x\right)}{4\mathopen{}\left(1+2\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right)\right)} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    \(f'(x) =\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{equation*} f'(x)=\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{19\sec\mathopen{}\left(x\right)}{4\mathopen{}\left(1+2\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right)\right)}\right)=\frac{19}{4}\frac{\frac{d}{dx}[\sec\mathopen{}\left(x\right)]\cdot (1+2\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right))-\frac{d}{dx}[1+2\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right)]\cdot \sec\mathopen{}\left(x\right)}{(1+2 \tan(x))^2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} =\frac{19}{4}\frac{\sec\mathopen{}\left(x\right)\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right)\cdot (1+2\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right))-2\sec^{2}\mathopen{}\left(x\right)\cdot \sec\mathopen{}\left(x\right)}{(1+2 \tan(x))^2}=\frac{19}{4}\frac{\sec(x)(\tan(x)+2(\tan^2(x)-\sec^2(x))}{(1+2 \tan(x))^2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} =\frac{19}{4}\frac{\sec(x)(\tan(x)-2)}{(1+2 \tan(x))^2}=\frac{19\sec\mathopen{}\left(x\right)\mathopen{}\left(\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right)-2\right)}{4\mathopen{}\left(1+2\tan\mathopen{}\left(x\right)\right)^{2}} \end{equation*}
