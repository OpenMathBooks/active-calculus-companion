    🔗

    🔗
    Hint.
    Left-Endpoint Approximation (Left Riemann Sum):
    🔗
    To use a left-endpoint approximation to estimate the area under a curve \(y=f(x)\) on an interval \([a,b]\) with `n` subintervals, we need to first identify a regular partition of the interval.
    🔗
    With \(\Delta x = \frac{b-a}{n}\) we can create a regular partition of subintervals of the form \([x_0,x_1], [x_1,x_2],...,[x_{n-1},x_n]\) where \(x_0=a, x_n=b\) and \(x_i = x_0+i\Delta x\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Then, construct a rectangle on each subinterval \([x_{i-1},x_i]\text{,}\) where the height of the rectangle is determined by the function value \(f(x_{i-1})\) at the left endpoint of the subinterval. Then, the area of each rectangle is \(f(x_{i-1})\Delta x\) and the approximation for the area, `A`, is given by
    🔗
    \(\begin{aligned} A\approx L_n \amp = f(x_0)\Delta x +f(x_1)\Delta x + ... +f(x_{n-1})\Delta x\\ \amp =\sum_{i=1}^n f(x_{i-1})\Delta x\\ \end{aligned}\)
