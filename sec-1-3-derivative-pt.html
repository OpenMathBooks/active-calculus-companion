    Section 1.3 The derivative of a function at a point

    Subsection 1.3.1 Foundations

    In this section of Active Calculus, you will take the algebraic skills you mastered in section 1.2 about limits and apply them to a very specific limit: the limit as \(h\) goes to 0 of the average rate of change. This will require the same sort of algebraic manipulation you practiced in section 1.2, so it would be a good idea to review Subsection 1.2.1 before doing additional practice in this section.

    🔗
